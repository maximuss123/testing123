Where

Katta, Malvan — Konkan, without the crowd

Malvan is known for Sindhudurg Fort, Malvani seafood, and some of the Konkan's quietest beaches. समर्थ नगरी sits inland of the coast road, close enough for a morning at Tarkarli and far enough for a settled, everyday life.

Sindhudurg's own airport at Chipi keeps the coast within a short regional flight; Goa is about 70 km by road, and the Mumbai–Goa highway runs through the district.
