The Project

समर्थ नगरी

Samarth means capable, self-sufficient — the idea behind this community. You hold the plan: build a home exactly as you've imagined it, or move into one that's already standing. Set in Katta on the outskirts of Malvan, समर्थ नगरी is a single gated layout of plots and ready homes, planned as one community rather than a patchwork of individual sales.

Why Here

The Konkan coast, at a human pace

Malvan means Sindhudurg Fort, Malvani fish curry, and beaches that haven't been overrun. It's close enough to Mumbai and Pune for a long weekend, and quiet enough that a long weekend is enough to decompress.

For a growing number of buyers, that's exactly the point — not a second home you visit once a year and forget, but a place you love going back to.
